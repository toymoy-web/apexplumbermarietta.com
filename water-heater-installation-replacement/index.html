Warning Signs

Signs you need Water Heater Installation in Marietta

No hot water at all

The most common reason for a same-day replacement call, especially with an older tank.

Rusty or discolored water

Usually a sign of internal tank corrosion rather than something a repair can fix.

Rumbling or popping noises

Sediment buildup at the bottom of the tank rumbling as the burner heats water through it.

Visible leaking at the base

Water pooling around the tank often means the tank itself has failed internally, not just a fitting.

Local Cause

Why this happens in Marietta

Standard tank water heaters typically last 10 to 15 years, and a large share of Marietta's housing stock is now old enough that original units installed decades ago are reaching or past that window. Moderately hard water in the Cobb County supply also shortens tank lifespan by accelerating sediment buildup at the bottom of the tank, which is the same mineral content that drives scale buildup in the copper supply lines feeding many of these homes. A tank nearing the end of that window is also more likely to fail without warning, which is why a no-hot-water call on an older unit gets treated as a same-day priority rather than scheduled out.

Diagnosis

How we find the actual problem

A technician inspects the tank, its venting, and the water and gas or electrical connections to confirm whether replacement is the right call versus a repair. For a tank over 10 years old with any of the symptoms above, replacement is usually the more cost-effective option than repairing a unit near the end of its service life.

Your Options

Repair options, honestly compared

Standard tank replacementThe most common choice, matched to the household's hot water demand and existing venting setup. Tankless conversionEndless hot water and a smaller footprint, though it requires confirming the home's gas line or electrical capacity can support it, see our servicing a tankless unit page. High-efficiency tank upgradeA direct tank-to-tank swap with a more efficient unit when a tankless conversion isn't the right fit for the home.

Pricing

What affects the price

Every job is quoted after diagnosis and approved by you before work starts. These are the real factors that move the price up or down:

Tank size and typeStandard tank capacity and fuel type (gas or electric) set the baseline for the unit itself.
Venting changesOlder homes sometimes need updated venting to meet current code, which adds to the job.
Access and locationA tank in a tight attic or crawlspace takes longer to remove and install than one in a garage or basement.
Common Questions

Water Heater Installation FAQ

How fast can you replace a water heater?

Most replacements complete the same day for calls that come in before early afternoon, since we carry common tank sizes on our trucks.

Do you haul away the old tank?

Yes, removal and disposal of the old unit is included with every replacement.

Is it worth repairing an old water heater instead of replacing it?

Once a tank is past 10 years old, a repair often just delays a replacement that's coming anyway. We'll tell you honestly which side of that line your unit is on before you commit to either option.

Can you upgrade my tank size during a replacement?

Yes, if the household has been running short on hot water, replacement is the natural point to size up, and we'll size the new unit to actual household demand rather than just matching what was there before.
