Warning Signs

Signs you need Garbage Disposal Repair in Marietta

A humming sound but no spinning

Usually a jammed impeller, often clearable without replacing the unit.

No sound at all when switched on

Can point to a tripped reset button, a failed motor, or an electrical supply issue depending on what else is affected.

Leaking from the bottom of the unit

Typically a failed seal, which usually means replacement rather than repair given the unit's age at that point.

Unusual grinding or rattling noises

Often a foreign object caught in the grinding chamber, worth checking before it damages the motor further.

A burning smell when the unit runs

Can indicate the motor is overheating, often from a jam that's straining it or a motor nearing failure, worth checking before it fails completely.

Local Cause

Why this happens in Marietta

Most garbage disposal failures come down to either a jam (something that shouldn't have gone down the drain) or a motor that's finally worn out after years of service, and telling the two apart before recommending a fix matters for cost. Units original to a home built in the 1990s or earlier are well past a typical 8 to 12 year service life, which is worth knowing before paying for a repair on a unit that's likely to fail again soon regardless.

Diagnosis

How we find the actual problem

We check whether the unit is jammed, tripped, or genuinely failed before recommending a repair or replacement, since a jam is a quick, low-cost fix while a failed motor or leaking seal usually means replacement is the more sensible option.

Common Questions

Garbage Disposal Repair FAQ

My disposal hums but won't spin, is it broken?

Not necessarily. A hum with no spin usually means the impeller is jammed, which is often clearable without replacing anything. We check this first before assuming the unit has failed.

How long do garbage disposals typically last?

Most units last 8 to 12 years with normal use. A unit that's leaking or has a failed motor at that age is usually more cost-effective to replace than repair.

What shouldn't go down a garbage disposal?

Fibrous vegetables, grease, coffee grounds, and bones are the most common causes of jams and premature wear. We're happy to go over the specifics for your unit during a service call.

Can I upgrade to a more powerful disposal during a replacement?

Yes, replacement is a natural point to upgrade horsepower or add noise-reducing features if the household has outgrown the original unit.

Do all disposals fit any kitchen sink setup?

Most standard sinks accommodate most disposal models, but mounting hardware and drain configuration can vary, which we confirm before installation rather than assuming compatibility.
