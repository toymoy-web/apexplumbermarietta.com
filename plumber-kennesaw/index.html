Local Conditions

The core problem driving Kennesaw service calls

Downtown Kennesaw's older homes, closer to the base of Kennesaw Mountain, carry aging copper similar to Marietta's own historic core. The newer subdivisions built up around KSU from the 1990s through the 2010s run PEX and modern copper instead. That means we genuinely see both ends of the housing-age spectrum on the same day here, not one dominant pattern like most of our other service areas. Which pattern applies to a given call usually comes down to a simple question. Is the home part of Kennesaw's original in-town footprint? Or is it part of the growth that followed the university's expansion from the 1990s onward?

Before We Arrive

If you have a burst pipe or active leak

Shut off the main valve

Stopping the water at the source matters more than anything else in the first few minutes.

Kill power to wet areas

If water is near an outlet or appliance, shut off power to that area at the breaker if it's safe to reach.

Clear the immediate area

Move furniture, rugs, and electronics away from the water while you wait, if it's safe to do so.

Local FAQ

Kennesaw questions

Is Kennesaw served by the same utility as Marietta?

No, not entirely. Kennesaw and Cobb County split water service by area, so which utility serves your specific address depends on where in Kennesaw you're located. We can confirm this during a service call.

Does my home's age matter for which plumbing issues to expect in Kennesaw?

Yes, more here than in most of our other areas. Downtown Kennesaw's older homes track with Historic Marietta's aging-copper pattern, while the newer KSU-adjacent subdivisions see the fixture and appliance issues typical of more recent construction.

Do you work with rental property managers near KSU?

Yes, we regularly work directly with property managers and landlords on rental properties near campus, including scheduling around tenant availability.

Is 24/7 emergency plumbing available in Kennesaw?

Yes, Kennesaw is inside our regular emergency dispatch coverage, day or night, weekday or weekend.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Kennesaw?

Yes, both Kennesaw ZIPs are covered, 30144, 30152, from the downtown Kennesaw historic core near the depot out through the Kennesaw State University-adjacent subdivisions and the Barrett Parkway growth corridor.
