Toilet Repair & Installation in Marietta, GA

A running, leaking, or clogged toilet is one of the most common calls we get in Marietta, and most of them are straightforward same-visit repairs. We also handle full toilet installation and replacement, including matching a new unit to an existing bathroom's rough-in dimensions.

Warning Signs

Signs you need Toilet Repair in Marietta

A toilet that runs continuously

Usually a worn flapper or fill valve failing to seal properly, a straightforward and inexpensive fix.

Water pooling at the base

Often a failed wax ring seal, requiring the toilet to be pulled and reseated.

Frequent clogging even with normal use

Can point to a partial blockage in the drain line beyond the toilet itself, worth checking with drain cleaning or a camera if it recurs.

A wobbly or unstable toilet

Usually loose mounting bolts or a failed wax ring, both straightforward to correct.

A phantom flush that happens on its own periodically

Usually a slow leak from the tank into the bowl through a worn flapper, wasting water even without anyone using the toilet.

Local Cause

Why this happens in Marietta

Older toilets in Marietta's pre-1990s housing stock often have original, aging fill valves and flappers well past their expected service life, while a toilet that's chronically clogging can point to a partial blockage further down the line rather than the toilet itself. Newer low-flow models installed since the 2000s tend to have fewer age-related component failures but can be more sensitive to what actually gets flushed.

Diagnosis

How we find the actual problem

Most toilet issues are diagnosed visually and by testing the fill and flush mechanisms directly. For a toilet that clogs repeatedly despite normal use, we'll check whether the issue is the fixture itself or a partial blockage further down the drain line.

Common Questions

Toilet Repair FAQ

Why does my toilet keep running after I flush?

Almost always a worn flapper that isn't sealing the tank properly, or a fill valve that isn't shutting off at the right water level. Both are quick, inexpensive fixes.

How often should a toilet be replaced?

A well-maintained toilet can last 20 years or more, but recurring cracks, chronic clogging, or an outdated high-flow model are all reasonable, honest reasons to replace rather than keep repairing it indefinitely.

Can a clogged toilet mean a bigger drain problem?

If plunging clears it and it doesn't recur, it's usually isolated. If it clogs repeatedly with normal use, that's worth investigating as a possible drain line issue rather than continuing to plunge it.

Do you install both standard and comfort-height toilets?

Yes, we install whatever height and style fits the bathroom and the household's preference, standard, comfort-height, or ADA-compliant models.

Can a running toilet actually raise my water bill noticeably?

Yes, a continuously running toilet can waste hundreds of gallons a day, which shows up clearly on a water bill and is one of the more cost-effective small repairs to fix promptly.
