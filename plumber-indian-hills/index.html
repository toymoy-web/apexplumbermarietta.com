Local Conditions

What makes Indian Hills plumbing different

The Indian Hills Country Club golf course anchors this neighborhood. The large, mature lots surrounding it mean two things for plumbing. First, copper supply lines from the 1960s-80s construction phase, now among the oldest we service. Second, decades of tree growth along the fairways and adjacent streets, which makes root intrusion into older clay or early PVC sewer laterals a genuine, recurring issue. Homes closest to the clubhouse and the oldest fairways tend to be the community's original construction. They're the ones most likely to still be running that first generation of copper. Newer custom builds added since the 1990s see it far less.

Before We Arrive

If you have a burst pipe or active leak

Shut off the main valve

Stopping the water at the source matters more than anything else in the first few minutes.

Kill power to wet areas

If water is near an outlet or appliance, shut off power to that area at the breaker if it's safe to reach.

Clear the immediate area

Move furniture, rugs, and electronics away from the water while you wait, if it's safe to do so.

Local FAQ

Indian Hills questions

Is Indian Hills' plumbing older than East Cobb's?

In many cases, yes. Indian Hills' core development dates to the 1960s-70s around the country club, a decade or more older than the 1970s-90s construction typical of the broader East Cobb subdivisions.

Do golf course-adjacent homes have different sewer issues?

The mature tree lines along the fairways and streets in Indian Hills mean root intrusion into older sewer laterals is a real, recurring issue here, more so than in newer parts of the county.

Should I have my Indian Hills home's original plumbing inspected?

If your home dates to the original 1960s-70s development phase and hasn't been repiped, a inspection is worth it given how far past typical service life that copper now is.

Is emergency service available in Indian Hills at night or on weekends?

Yes, emergency dispatch runs around the clock in Indian Hills, the same as our broader Marietta-area coverage.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Indian Hills?

Yes, the entire Indian Hills community sits within ZIP 30067, covering the Indian Hills Country Club community itself and the Atlanta Country Club-adjacent streets around it.
