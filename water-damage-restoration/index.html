Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Marietta, GA

Water damage restoration is a different service than plumbing repair: once the pipe or appliance causing the damage is fixed, drying and restoring the affected structure and contents is its own job. We handle structural drying, dehumidification, and restoration for Marietta homes after a leak, burst pipe, or flooding event.

Warning Signs

Signs you need Water Damage Restoration in Marietta

Visible standing water

Water pooling on floors after a pipe failure or appliance malfunction needs extraction before drying can begin.

Damp drywall or a musty odor

Moisture trapped inside a wall cavity often shows up as odor or discoloration before visible damage appears.

Warped or buckling flooring

Water absorbed into wood or laminate flooring causes visible warping, a sign restoration is needed beyond simple drying.

Visible mold growth

Untreated moisture in Georgia's humid climate can develop mold within 24 to 48 hours, making fast response genuinely time-sensitive.

Local Cause

Why this happens in Marietta

In Marietta, water damage restoration calls most often follow a burst pipe, a failed water heater, or a slab leak that went unnoticed long enough to soak flooring or drywall. Georgia's humid climate also means standing moisture left untreated can lead to mold growth faster than in a drier region, making prompt drying especially important here.

Diagnosis

How we find the actual problem

We assess the extent of water intrusion using moisture meters to identify affected materials that aren't visibly wet yet, since water travels behind walls and under flooring further than what's immediately visible. This assessment determines the drying plan and whether any materials need to be removed rather than dried in place.

Your Options

What affects the price

Common Questions

Water Damage Restoration FAQ

Is water damage restoration covered by homeowners insurance?

Often, yes, particularly for sudden events like a burst pipe, though coverage details vary by policy. We can document the damage for your claim, but coverage decisions go through your insurer.

How long does structural drying take?

Most drying takes 3 to 5 days depending on the extent of water intrusion and the materials affected, verified with moisture meter readings rather than a fixed timeline.

Do you also fix the plumbing issue that caused the damage?

If the cause was a plumbing repair issue, like a burst pipe, that repair is handled through our standard plumbing line, a separate service from the drying and restoration work itself.

Before We Arrive

What to do while you wait

Shut off the water source

If the water is still actively flowing, shutting off the main or the specific fixture's supply valve stops the source before restoration can meaningfully begin.

Shut off power to affected areas if safe

Standing water near outlets or appliances is an electrocution risk; shut off power at the breaker if you can do so without stepping into the water.

Move valuables and furniture

Get furniture, electronics, and valuables away from standing water while you wait, if it's safe to do so.

Document before cleanup begins

Photos of the standing water and visible damage are useful for an insurance claim, taken before any extraction or drying starts.
