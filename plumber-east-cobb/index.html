Before We Arrive

If you have a burst pipe or active leak

Shut off the main valve

Stopping the water at the source matters more than anything else in the first few minutes.

Kill power to wet areas

If water is near an outlet or appliance, shut off power to that area at the breaker if it's safe to reach.

Clear the immediate area

Move furniture, rugs, and electronics away from the water while you wait, if it's safe to do so.

Local FAQ

East Cobb questions

Why do so many East Cobb homes get slab leaks?

Most of East Cobb was built in the 1970s through 1990s on copper supply lines that are now 30 to 50 years old, well past typical service life. That age is the single biggest driver of the slab leaks we see in this specific area.

Can tree roots really damage a sewer line in a subdivision like EastHampton?

Yes. Trees planted alongside these homes in the 1970s-80s have had decades to develop root systems, and root intrusion into aging clay or early PVC sewer laterals is a real, recurring issue in mature subdivisions like this one.

Is a repipe worth it if I've only had one leak?

One leak in a home this age is usually just a repair. A second leak within a year or two is when a full repipe typically starts making more financial sense than continuing to chase individual leaks.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency plumbing in East Cobb?

Do you cover every ZIP code in East Cobb?
