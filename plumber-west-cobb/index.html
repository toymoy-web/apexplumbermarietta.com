Local Conditions

How West Cobb's construction era shapes what we see

PEX and newer copper are common in West Cobb's more recent construction. That means the pinhole and slab leaks that dominate calls in older parts of the county are less frequent here. What we see instead tracks with newer-construction homes generally: fixture and appliance issues rather than supply-line age. The rolling terrain and newer stormwater infrastructure in this part of the county also mean fewer standing-water drainage issues. Those issues show up more in some flatter, older neighborhoods. Continued infill building near Dallas Highway also means we're regularly called for new-construction hookups and inspections here. That type of call is rare in the county's older, fully built-out neighborhoods.

Warning Signs

Signs West Cobb homeowners should know

A garbage disposal that hums but won't turn

One of the more common calls from West Cobb's newer-construction kitchens, distinct from the leak-driven calls further east.

A dripping or loose faucet cartridge

Fixture wear on newer installations, rather than the corrosion issues seen in older copper or galvanized systems.

A toilet that runs intermittently

Typically a flapper or fill valve issue on newer fixtures, an easy same-visit repair.

New-construction inspection requests

Continued building activity off Dallas Highway means plumbing inspections and new hookups are a regular part of what we do in this area.

A slow-to-fill dishwasher or washing machine

More often a supply valve or connection issue on newer plumbing than the pressure-loss problems seen in older, corroded systems.

Before We Arrive

If you have a burst pipe or active leak

Shut off the main valve

Stopping the water at the source matters more than anything else in the first few minutes.

Kill power to wet areas

If water is near an outlet or appliance, shut off power to that area at the breaker if it's safe to reach.

Clear the immediate area

Move furniture, rugs, and electronics away from the water while you wait, if it's safe to do so.

Local FAQ

West Cobb questions

Does West Cobb have the same slab leak issues as East Cobb?

Less often. West Cobb's housing stock skews newer, with more PEX and modern copper than East Cobb's 1970s-90s construction, so we see fewer age-related supply line failures here.

Do you handle plumbing for new construction in West Cobb?

Yes, the continued growth around Dallas Highway means new-construction inspections and hookups are a regular part of our West Cobb work, not just repair calls.

Is well water common in any part of West Cobb?

The vast majority of West Cobb is served by the Cobb County Water System rather than private wells, though we're happy to confirm your specific situation if you're unsure.

Do you offer emergency plumbing service in West Cobb?

Yes, 24/7 emergency dispatch covers West Cobb the same as the rest of our Marietta-area coverage.

Do you cover every ZIP code in West Cobb?
