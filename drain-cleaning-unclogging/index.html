Emergency Drain Cleaning & Unclogging in Marietta, GA

A slow or fully clogged drain rarely fixes itself, and in Marietta homes it's often a symptom of something further down the line than a store-bought drain cleaner can reach. We clear clogged kitchen sinks, bathroom drains, and main sewer lines using cable snaking and hydro jetting, and we diagnose the actual blockage before we quote a fix.

Warning Signs

Signs you need Drain Cleaning in Marietta

Slow-draining sink or tub

Water backs up gradually instead of draining, often the first sign of a partial clog.

Gurgling sounds

Air trapped behind a blockage escapes back up the drain, especially after running the washing machine or dishwasher.

Multiple fixtures backing up at once

A shower backing up when the washing machine drains usually points to a main line clog, not a single fixture.

Recurring clogs in the same drain

A clog that keeps coming back after snaking usually means a structural issue like a pipe belly or root intrusion, not a fresh blockage.

Local Cause

Why this happens in Marietta

In the older sections of Marietta, cast-iron waste lines from the 1970s and earlier have had decades to accumulate scale and root intrusion from mature trees planted around the same time as the houses themselves. In newer construction, clogs are more often grease, hair, and soap buildup in PVC lines that haven't had time to develop structural problems yet.

Diagnosis

How we find the actual problem

We start with a hand-fed cable snake for a straightforward single-fixture clog. When a clog is recurring, affects multiple fixtures, or doesn't respond to snaking, we run a sewer camera inspection to see the actual condition of the line, whether that's root intrusion, a bellied pipe, or scale buildup narrowing the interior diameter.

Your Options

Repair options, honestly compared

Cable snakingThe right call for a single, localized clog with no signs of a structural problem. Hydro jettingOur a high-pressure line clearing service clears grease, scale, and root intrusion that a cable can't fully remove, and it's the more thorough option for a recurring clog. Common Questions

Drain Cleaning FAQ

How do you know if it's one drain or the main line?

A single slow drain is usually isolated to that fixture. If more than one fixture backs up at the same time, or backs up when another one runs, that points to the main line and changes how we approach it.

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?

We check the pipe material and condition first, including with a camera when there's any doubt. Hydro jetting is adjusted for pipe age and condition rather than run at one fixed pressure for every job.

Can tree roots really get into a sewer line?

Yes, and it's one of the more common causes of a recurring clog in Marietta's older neighborhoods with mature trees planted decades ago, close to the same age as the clay or early PVC lines underneath them.

Will drain cleaning damage my pipes?

Cable snaking and hydro jetting are both standard, non-destructive methods when matched to the pipe's actual material and condition, which is exactly why we check that first rather than running one setting for every job.
