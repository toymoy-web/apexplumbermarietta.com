Local Cause

Why this happens in Marietta

New installation work in Marietta ranges from a single new fixture in an existing bathroom to full rough-in plumbing for an addition or renovation, and the permitting requirements differ meaningfully between the two. Older homes taking on new installation work sometimes also need an assessment of whether existing supply lines have the capacity for the added fixtures.

Diagnosis

How we find the actual problem

We review the project scope and existing plumbing layout to plan the new installation, including confirming what permits are required and coordinating inspection timing with the rest of the project.

Every job is quoted after diagnosis and approved by you before work starts. These are the real factors that move the price up or down:

Common Questions

Plumbing Installation FAQ

Do I need a permit to add a bathroom?

In most cases, yes. New plumbing installation for an added bathroom typically requires a permit through the Cobb County Community Development Agency, which we coordinate as part of the job.

Can you work alongside my general contractor on a renovation?

Yes, we regularly coordinate plumbing installation timing with general contractors on larger renovation and addition projects.

How far in advance should I schedule new construction plumbing?

As early as possible in the project timeline, since rough-in plumbing needs to happen before walls are closed and usually needs to align closely with other trades' schedules.

Can you install plumbing for an outdoor kitchen or pool house?

Yes, we handle exterior plumbing installation for outdoor structures, including winterization considerations specific to Georgia's climate and occasional winter freezes.

Do you handle plumbing for a full home renovation?

Yes, we coordinate plumbing installation and relocation for full renovations, working alongside your general contractor's timeline for framing, drywall, and finishing stages.

Do you provide the fixtures, or do I supply them?

Either works. We can supply standard fixtures as part of the installation, or install fixtures you've already purchased, whichever fits your project and budget.
