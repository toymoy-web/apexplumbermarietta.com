Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026. This policy explains what information Marietta Apex Plumbing collects and how it's used.

Information We Collect

When you call, email, or otherwise contact Marietta Apex Plumbing, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, service address, and details about the plumbing issue you're contacting us about. Call recordings may be used for quality assurance and to route emergency dispatch accurately.

How We Use Information

Information you provide is used to schedule and perform the plumbing services you request, to follow up on completed work, and to respond to questions. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Call Tracking

Phone numbers shown on this site may be call-tracking numbers used to measure which marketing channels generate calls. Calls to these numbers are routed directly to Marietta Apex Plumbing and may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Cookies & Website Data

This website may use standard analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. This data is used in aggregate to improve the site and is not used to personally identify visitors beyond what's described above.

Your Choices

You can ask us to delete personal information we hold about you, subject to any records we're required to retain for legal, licensing, or warranty purposes, by contacting us at contact@apexplumbermarietta.com.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be directed to contact@apexplumbermarietta.com or (770) 214-4545.
