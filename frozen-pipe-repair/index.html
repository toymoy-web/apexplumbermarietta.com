Local Cause

Why this happens in Marietta

Because deep freezes are occasional rather than constant here, many Marietta homes have pipe runs in crawl spaces or exterior walls that were never insulated the way they would be in a colder climate, which is exactly what makes them vulnerable when a real cold snap does hit.

Diagnosis

How we find the actual problem

For a suspected frozen pipe, we locate the frozen section and assess whether it can be safely thawed without damage, or whether it's already burst and needs repair rather than thawing. Any resulting repair follows the Georgia State Minimum Standard Plumbing Code, based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC).

Common Questions

Frozen Pipe Repair FAQ

Should I try to thaw a frozen pipe myself?

Careful, gradual warming with a hair dryer on an accessible section is generally safe; open flame or aggressive heat sources risk causing a burst. When in doubt, especially for an inaccessible section, it's worth having it done professionally.

How common are frozen pipes in Georgia?

Far less common than in colder states, but occasional hard freezes do happen, and homes here are less likely to have pipes insulated for it, which is exactly what makes the ones that do occur more damaging.

Can I prevent frozen pipes before next winter?

Yes. Insulating exposed pipe in crawl spaces and exterior walls, and letting faucets drip during a hard freeze, both meaningfully reduce the risk.

Does homeowners insurance cover frozen pipe damage?

Often, yes, particularly for the resulting burst and water damage, though coverage specifics vary by policy and whether reasonable preventive steps were taken.

Before We Arrive

What to do while you wait

Shut off the water if a pipe has already burst

Stopping the source at the main valve is the priority if thawing has revealed a burst section.

Never use an open flame to thaw a pipe

A blowtorch or open flame on a pipe is a fire hazard and can damage the pipe further; use a hair dryer or heat tape designed for the purpose instead.

Keep the faucet open during thawing

Opening the fixture the frozen line feeds lets water and steam escape as the ice melts, reducing pressure buildup.

Call promptly if you can't locate the frozen section

An inaccessible frozen pipe, especially inside a wall, is worth a professional assessment rather than guessing at where to apply heat.
